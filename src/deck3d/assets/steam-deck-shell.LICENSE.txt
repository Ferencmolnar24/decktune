steam-deck-shell.bin

Steam Deck external shell geometry by Valve Corporation, from the official Steam Deck CAD release
(https://gitlab.steamos.cloud/SteamHardware/SteamDeck, steamdeck_stl_20220202.stl), licensed under
Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/).

Changes made by DeckTune: re-oriented and scaled for the 3D repair viewer, split into front shell,
controls and back cover at a plane near the shell seam, vertex positions quantized to 16-bit and
packed into a compact binary format. This adapted file is shared under the same license.
